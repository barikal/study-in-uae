# Study-in-UAE

**Study-in-UAE** is a strategic initiative to attract Turkish students to private universities in the United Arab Emirates (UAE).  
The project focuses on research, data analysis, and targeted outreach to connect Turkish students with quality academic opportunities in the UAE.  

---

## Goals
- Analyze **Turkish higher education demand** (YKS exam data, student preferences, placement trends).  
- Map **UAE private universities** and their top programs.  
- Build a **Turkey–UAE university correspondence table** for better program alignment.  
- Develop **outreach strategies** to engage Turkish students (starting with those applying to Cyprus).  
- Track **documents, prompts, and datasets** in a reproducible, version-controlled way.
