    UAE Undergraduate Business Programs Comparison

    This repository contains research reports comparing undergraduate Business and Management programs at private universities in the United Arab Emirates.
    The reports include program names, tuition fees in AED & USD, and fee types (per year / full course / per credit hour), as well as a summary of additional education-related costs.

    File Naming Convention

    Reports follow the format:

    uae_business_programs_comparison_YYYYMMDD_vN.md

    • YYYYMMDD → Date of the report (e.g., 20250819 for 19 Aug 2025).
    • vN → Version number (e.g., v1, v2).
    • A symlink/alias file named uae_business_programs_comparison_latest.md always points to the most recent version.

    Examples

    • uae_business_programs_comparison_20250819_v1.md → First draft saved Aug 19, 2025.
    • uae_business_programs_comparison_20250901_v2.md → Updated on Sept 1, 2025.
    • uae_business_programs_comparison_latest.md → Current/latest report.

    Contents

    Each report contains:

    1. Comparison Table
    2. University
    3. Program(s)
    4. Tuition fees in AED & USD

    5. Fee type (per year, per course, or per credit hour with annual estimate)

    6. Additional Costs

    7. Visa & residency fees
    8. Health insurance
    9. Registration & admin fees

    10. Accommodation & living expenses

    11. Source Citations

    12. Direct links to official university websites or reliable sources for verification.

    Usage

    • For Researchers/Advisors: Quickly compare tuition structures across UAE universities.
    • For Students/Parents: Understand the cost structure before applying.
    • For Partners/Institutions: Use as background for recruitment strategies and outreach.

    Updates

    • Reports will be updated periodically as universities revise their fee schedules.
    • Major updates (e.g., new academic year fees, program changes) will be tracked with a new date + version tag.
    • Minor corrections (typos, formatting) may increment version only.
