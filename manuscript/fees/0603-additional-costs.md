
### Additional Costs (summary)

- Student visa & residency: ≈ AED 5,300 (first year incl. refundable deposit; +VAT)
- Health insurance: AED 3,000–6,000 per year depending on coverage  
- Accommodation: 
  - AED 3,000–5,250/month at student residences (The Myriad, DIAC)
  - AED 17,000–28,000 per year (AUD on-campus)
- Living expenses: approx. AED 4,500–6,500/month
- Registration/admin/tech fees: e.g., AED 2,700 per year (Sorbonne AD); AED 1,500–1,650 per course (AUS tech/lab fees)
